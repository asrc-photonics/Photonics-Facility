The ASRC Photonics Shared Facility hosts students at various levels — from high-school to undergraduate — to provide hands-on research experience in optics and photonics.  

Through summer programs, internships, and mentorship opportunities, students gain exposure to advanced equipment, collaborative projects, and the excitement of scientific discovery.
